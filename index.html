Our Vision

🤝

Our Mission

To promote consultation, transparent leadership, responsible use of public resources and constructive engagement on local development priorities.

Our Manifesto

Proposed areas of focus for discussion with residents. Final commitments should reflect local needs and available resources.

01

Roads & Accessibility

Engage relevant authorities on local road maintenance, access routes and safer movement within the ward.

02

Water Access

Support dialogue on water access, conservation and practical community water projects.

03

Education

Promote fair information on bursary opportunities and advocate for support for learners in need.

04

Youth Opportunities

Encourage skills development, sports, mentorship and access to information about employment and enterprise opportunities.

05

Health & Community Welfare

Raise community health-service concerns with the relevant authorities and encourage public participation in local welfare initiatives.

06

Accountability

Encourage public participation, clear communication and responsible oversight of ward-level development priorities.
